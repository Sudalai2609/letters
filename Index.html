How a letter is made

Choose each layer, then write the words yourself. Every piece is designed to go together.

  1. Pick the style of your letterFrom Victorian restraint to a shy first confession.
  2. Choose an envelope and how it opensCrack a wax seal, scratch off a heart, or unlock a safe.
  3. Stamp itVintage cameos, monograms, even a stamp made from your own photo.
  4. Add the finishing touchesA vinyl record from your own files, polaroids, scents, a hidden P.S.
  5. Send it by emailThey get a note in their inbox and a private link to open it.
